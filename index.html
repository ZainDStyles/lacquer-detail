Good to know

Before you book.

How long will you have my car?

A Wash takes about two hours, so you can wait. Enhance is a full day. Ceramic takes two to three days, because the coating needs 24 hours indoors to cure before it sees rain.

Can you collect the car?

Yes. We collect and return within 15 km of the workshop for free on Enhance and Ceramic bookings. For a Wash it's a flat $25.

Will polishing damage my paint?

Not the way we do it. We read every panel with a paint gauge first and use the gentlest pad and compound that removes the defect. If a panel is too thin to polish safely, we tell you and leave it.

How do I look after a ceramic coat?

Wait a week before the first wash, then hand wash with a pH-neutral shampoo. Skip automatic brush washes. Come back once a year for a free coating check.

When do I pay?

A 20% deposit holds your slot. You pay the rest at pick-up, after you've walked round the car with us.
